Clog Alert (Grade 11 capstone)

A smart drainage system that detects blockages and helps manage minor flooding. If the water sensor sees no water flowing into the drain, the system assumes a clog and turns on a pump to send floodwater back into the drainage until the blockage can be cleared by hand. It is meant for flood-prone areas and works all year.

My role

[Your role in the group]

Key features

  • Water sensor near the drainage outlet
  • Automatic pump when a clog is detected
  • Planned buzzer alarm, which we could not finish because of a technical issue with the buzzer

Tools and platforms

  • Arduino
  • Soil moisture sensor
  • Water pump

What I would improve

  • Add a mesh filter and regular sensor maintenance to prevent false readings
  • Use a solar panel for continuous power
  • Add a rainfall sensor to avoid false clog alerts
  • Use a larger pump and send an alert when water exceeds pump capacity
  • Move the electronics to an elevated waterproof compartment, with optional Bluetooth or Wi-Fi monitoring

Personal journey

Studying at Mapúa is hard, and I won't pretend otherwise. I went from being an achiever in Senior High School to barely understanding simple concepts, and I'll admit I haven't always felt like myself.

Challenges

Courses, outputs, and understanding new concepts at a much faster pace have all been difficult. I was a grade-conscious high schooler who pushed herself to the limit until she hit a wall. In college, I picked up a mindset I never expected: "ang mahalaga, may napasa" (what matters is that something got submitted). Passing courses still matters, but I'm glad I'm no longer the person who pushes until she breaks.

Growth

After some time of understanding myself and cutting myself some slack, my mindset changed. What matters is that I grow, learn, and still reach my dream. Every challenge in a course or an output helps shape my future. I'll keep going through hardships in this university, and I'm willing to overcome them and learn my way around.
